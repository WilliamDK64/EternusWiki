###### Glaive
Iris is a glaive created by [[Non-Player Characters/Iris Mentis]] and currently in the possession of [[Player Characters/Xenevieve Mentis-Sara]]. Its shaft is made out of Yuphrali redwood and the blade at its tip is a sliver of [[Creatures/Sun Drakes\|Sun Drake]] tooth, belonging to [[Non-Player Characters/Siris]] Khan.
