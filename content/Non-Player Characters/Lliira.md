###### (she/her) - NPC
Lliira of Three is the goddess of joy and celebration, and is one of the [[Three Sisters]]. She currently lives on the [[Isle of the Three Sisters]] and is married to [[Non-Player Characters/Eitr Sara]].

As one of the Three Sisters, she is the demigod child of [[Non-Player Characters/Lathander]].
