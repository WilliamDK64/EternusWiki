###### Dragon
Elder dragons are the highest being of creation convoked by [[Non-Player Characters/Urvox]], tasked with being its adjudicators in the [[Convoker’s War]]. They can grow to be larger than all other dragons, have four wings, and often have many colours mixed in between their shining bronze and gold scales. Elder dragons can breathe any element as their breath weapon, and do so by speaking the [[Languages/Old Tongue]] fluently.
