###### Continent
Alfheim is the eastern continent of Eternus. Western Alfheim is home to the Free Cities of the [[Impious Alliance]], including [[Locations/Emperia]], [[Mædway]], and [[Faeleen]]. Meanwhile, eastern Alfheim is considered the east edge of the world, as the materiality of the continent begins to blend into the [[Locations/Feywild]].

East Alfheim was once the location of the great mage city of [[Locations/Sigil]], before it ascended to the [[Astral Plane]] to orbit [[Locations/Carceri]] after [[Events/Agon#The Second Agon\|The Second Agon]]. As it flew away, [[Non-Player Characters/Pölark]] unleashed the rage of [[Groups/The Triad]] upon it, missing the city but creating a sprawling black desert across east Alfheim known as [[Locations/Landfall]]. From this desert came hordes of [[Nightshades]] created by Pölark, which upon finding no Sigil in sight instead wrought havoc upon the rest of the continent, becoming one of the main issues during the [[Events/Time of Strife]].
