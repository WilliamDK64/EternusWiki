###### (he/him) - NPC
Jarred is a human [[Creatures/Werewolves\|Werewolf]] bonesmith, and is part of [[Groups/The Pack]]. He created [[Items/Weapons/Veilpiercer]] for [[Player Characters/Xenevieve Mentis-Sara\|Xenevieve Mentis]], as well as creating King [[Non-Player Characters/Theodore Sara]]'s engagement ring out of [[Therizo]] bone. He also created the base weapons for [[Queen's Hunt]] and [[Chrysalis]], which were then enchanted by [[Cassandra Dannell]] and given as a gift from [[Player Characters/Xenevieve Mentis-Sara\|Xenevieve Mentis]] to Lord [[Non-Player Characters/Favon Atalanta]] and his wife Lady [[Non-Player Characters/Cil'ahn of Shrike]].
